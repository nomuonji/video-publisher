Data Deletion Instructions

We respect your privacy. If you wish to remove your data from our system, you can do so by following these steps:

Option 1: Disconnect via Dashboard

  1. Log in to our application.
  2. Navigate to the Settings or Connections page.
  3. Find the TikTok section and click the Disconnect or Unlink button.
  4. This action will immediately remove your access tokens and associated profile information from our database.

Option 2: Remove Access via TikTok

  1. Open the TikTok app or website.
  2. Go to Settings and Privacy > Security > Manage App Permissions.
  3. Find our app in the list and select Remove Access.

If you have any issues, please contact our support team.
