Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to our application. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect

When you choose to connect your TikTok account, we collect the following information:

  • Profile Information: Your TikTok username, display name, and avatar image.
  • Authentication Data: Tokens required to authenticate with the TikTok API on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Display your connected account status within our dashboard.
  • Upload and publish videos to your TikTok account as requested by you.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required to function (e.g., sending video data to TikTok's servers for publication) or as required by law.

5. Your Rights

You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time through our application settings. Upon disconnection, we will remove your access tokens from our system.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
